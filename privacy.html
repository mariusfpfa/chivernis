Privacy Policy — Chivernis

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Chivernis is a personal expense-tracking tool operated by a private individual

for their own use. It is not offered as a service to other people.

Who runs this

Marius — contact: mariusf.pfa@gmail.com

What data is accessed

With the account holder's explicit consent, given through their own bank's

authorisation flow, Chivernis reads:

  • account identifiers (IBAN, account name, currency, product type)
  • account balances
  • transaction history — dates, amounts, counterparty names, merchant category

codes, and payment reference text

Access is read-only. Chivernis cannot initiate payments or move money.

How access is obtained

Bank access is provided through Enable Banking Oy, a licensed account

information service provider, under the EU Payment Services Directive (PSD2).

Chivernis never sees or stores bank credentials — authentication happens

entirely on the bank's own pages.

What the data is used for

Categorising spending, calculating budgets, and displaying personal financial

summaries to the account holder. Nothing else.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. The data is not sold, published, shared, or used for advertising,

profiling, or training machine-learning models. The only third party involved

is Enable Banking, which transmits the data from the bank.

Where it is stored

Locally, on hardware controlled by the operator. Data is retained only for as

long as it is useful for personal budgeting and can be deleted at any time.

Consent and withdrawal

Bank access expires automatically as required by PSD2 — at most 90 days — after

which it must be re-authorised. Consent can be withdrawn at any time from the

bank's own app or by contacting the address above.

Your rights

As the sole data subject is also the operator, GDPR rights of access,

rectification, erasure, and portability are exercised directly.
