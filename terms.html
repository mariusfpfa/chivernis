Last updated: 30 July 2026

What this is

Chivernis is a private, personal expense-tracking and budgeting tool. It is

operated by a private individual for their own use and is not offered to the

public, sold, or provided as a service to anyone else.

Access to bank data

Account information is retrieved through Enable Banking Oy, a licensed account

information service provider, under PSD2. Access is read-only and requires

explicit authorisation by the account holder through their bank's own

authentication flow. Chivernis cannot initiate payments or transfer funds.

No financial advice

Chivernis displays and categorises the account holder's own transaction data.

Nothing it produces is financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

Accuracy

Figures are derived from data supplied by banks and may be incomplete, delayed,

or inaccurate. Transaction categorisation is automated and will sometimes be

wrong. The bank's own statements remain the authoritative record.

Availability

Provided as-is, with no guarantee of availability, correctness, or fitness for

any purpose. Bank connections may break at any time, and consent expires at

least every 90 days under PSD2.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, no liability is accepted for any loss

arising from use of this tool or from reliance on the figures it displays.
