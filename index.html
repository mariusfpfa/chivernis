Chivernis

A personal expense-tracking and budgeting tool.

Chivernis reads balances and transaction history from its operator's own bank accounts, with their explicit consent, to categorise spending and track budgets. Access is read-only and obtained through Enable Banking Oy, a licensed account information service provider, under PSD2.

It is a private tool, not a service offered to the public.
